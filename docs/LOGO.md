# Howzat logo

The forest-green symbol combines a cricket wicket with coding braces. The live
Howzat wordmark remains beside it in the header for crisp text at every size.

Assets in `frontend/public/assets/`:

- `howzat-logo.png`: original high-resolution transparent master (1254 × 1254).
- `howzat-mark-128.png`: transparent header icon (128 × 128), displayed at 32 px.
- `howzat-icon-64.png`: transparent browser favicon (64 × 64).

The master was generated with the built-in image generation tool. The smaller
PNG assets were resized from it with transparency retained. This is a raster
logo, not an SVG source. All assets are served locally.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create one refined, distinctive logo SYMBOL for HOWZAT, a cricket scoring compiler. A minimal flat vector-style emblem combining a cricket wicket with programming curly braces: two strong outer curly-brace silhouettes frame a clean central upright stump, with a simple horizontal bail near the top unifying the wicket. Readable as both cricket stumps and a compact coding mark. The outline may subtly suggest a capital H. Elegant precise geometric construction, balanced medium-bold stroke weight, generous open counters that remain readable at 24 px. ONE solid ink color only: deep forest green #28532A. Absolutely transparent background. Square composition, centered, symbol fills about 82% of canvas with even small margins. No lettering, no words, no wordmark, no cricket bat, no ball, no gradients, no shadows, no paper texture, no 3D, no mockup, no border frame, no tiny details. Deliver a single isolated professional brand icon, suitable for a cream and forest-green website header and favicon.
